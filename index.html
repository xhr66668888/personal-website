I am a Ph.D. student in Computer Science at the University of Oklahoma, where I'm fortunate to work with Dr. Ziming Liu. My recent research explores AI for Health and multimodal learning, focusing on representation learning and machine learning for complex physiological and behavioral biosensors (such as EEG, articulatory kinematics, and in-home/wearable sensing), aiming to build interpretable models for early cognitive screening, speech motor assessment, and longitudinal condition management. Before my Ph.D., I received my M.S. in Electrical Engineering from the University of Washington and my B.S. in Mathematics from The Ohio State University.
